# Code of Conduct

Wren is a technical project. This code exists to keep collaboration here
productive and free of harassment — nothing more.

## Scope

This code applies **only within the project's own spaces**: this repository,
its issues and pull requests, and any official Wren communication channels.

It does **not** govern what contributors do, say, or believe outside the
project. Members' personal, political, philosophical, or religious views — and
their conduct in other communities — are **none of this project's business** and
are never grounds for action here. You are judged solely by your conduct and
your contributions within the project's spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Keep discussion on-topic, technical, and in good faith.
- Be respectful and constructive, even in disagreement.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on technical direction, and that
  "no" is a valid answer to a contribution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Within the project's spaces:

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks against another participant.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, deliberate derailing, or spam.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or imagery.

## Enforcement

If someone's behavior **in the project's spaces** violates this code, report it
privately to **hello@rafaelvieiras.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and
in good faith.

Maintainers may respond with actions scoped to project participation — for
example editing or removing offending comments/contributions, or restricting a
participant's access to the project's spaces — proportionate to the situation.
Enforcement is limited to the project; it never extends to a person's life or
activities outside it.
